About the portal

ComplainEase is the grievance portal for the corporation. A resident registers, files a complaint against one of the published departments and marks its location. The cell assigns it to an officer in that department. The officer records the action taken and attaches proof of work, and only then can the complaint be marked resolved. The resident closes it or sends it back. Nothing is deleted on the way, so the whole history sits against the reference number.

An officer can register here, but the account does nothing until the cell approves it and issues a staff number.
